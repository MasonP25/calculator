# **Mason's Arcade**

### A collection of 97 browser-based games — no frameworks, no dependencies, just pure HTML/CSS/JS.

[**Play Now**](https://masonp25.github.io/calculator/game-site/)

---

</div>

## What's Inside

- **97 Games** across solo, local multiplayer, and online multiplayer
- **Online Multiplayer** with real-time WebSocket rooms and chat
- **Global Leaderboards** powered by Firebase
- **User Accounts** to track scores, coins, and progress across devices
- **Coin Economy** — earn coins from leaderboard placements, daily challenges, and streaks
- **Avatar System** with 60+ items including hats, hair, faces, and shirts
- **Friends System** with friend requests, online status, and activity tracking
- **Daily Challenges & Weekly Quests** with bonus rewards
- **Leveling & XP** system with rank progression
- **Badges & Achievements** for milestones
- **Item Trading & Gifting** between players
- **Game Ratings** — rate and review games
- **Game Invites** — invite friends to play
- **Live Activity Feed** showing leaderboard records and achievements
- **Notifications** for friend requests, score changes, and more
- **Daily Login Streaks** with coin rewards
- **25 Themes** that apply across every game
- **Sound Effects** with mute toggle
- **Animated Page Transitions**

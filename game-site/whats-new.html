What's New

A few fresh changes to make the arcade feel a little more alive.

🐾

Pet System v2 Reworked

Your pet now walks across the homepage with proper physics. Drag it anywhere and it falls, bounces, and lands on top of game cards. It rides each card row as you scroll, and lifts up when you hover the card it's standing on.

✨

Visual Refresh

Cleaner title with a slower glitch and faster shimmer, refined typography, and smoother card hover animations. The ARCADE wordmark, magnetic buttons, and ambient particle field all got a polish pass.

🤖

Smarter Assistant

The chat assistant is now routed through a private backend so it stays fast and reliable without exposing keys. It also pulls in your live coin balance, level progress, and current daily challenge for context-aware answers.

🎥

Arcade Vids Upload Wizard

The Arcade Vids card now opens a clean step-by-step uploader: title, content, thumbnail, confirm. Drag-drop or paste a link, pick a thumbnail, and your video lands in the community feed.

Pet System v2

Your pet now walks across the homepage with proper physics. Drag it anywhere and it falls, bounces, and lands on top of game cards. It rides each card row as you scroll, and lifts up when you hover the card it's standing on.

Adopt one on your profile ✨

Visual Refresh

An ongoing polish pass across the whole site — refined typography, smoother card hover, animated reveals, magnetic buttons, particle field, custom scrollbar, page transitions, and more.

Smarter Assistant

The chat assistant is now routed through a private backend so it stays fast and reliable. It also pulls in your live coin balance, level progress, and daily challenge for context-aware answers.

The Arcade Vids card now opens a clean step-by-step uploader: title, content, thumbnail, confirm. Drag-drop a file or paste a link, pick a thumbnail, and your video lands in the community feed.

Try it on Arcade Vids 💬

Everything you need to know about Mason's Arcade

Mason's Arcade is a free online gaming platform with 130+ browser games you can play instantly — no downloads or installations needed. Just pick a game and start playing right in your browser.
You earn coins by playing games, spinning the daily wheel, and completing challenges. Spend them in the Shop on avatar items like hats, hairstyles, shirts, and nametags. Coins have no real-money value — they're just for fun and customization.
The Hall of Fame is our global leaderboard system that tracks the best scores across all games. Compete with other players and see if you can claim the top spot.
Visit the Shop to browse and buy hats, hairstyles, shirts, face accessories, and nametag styles using your earned coins. Equip items to show off your look across the site and in multiplayer games.

We recommend Chrome, Edge, or any Chromium-based browser for the best experience. Firefox and Safari will work for most games, but some features may perform better on Chromium browsers.
There are two ways to add friends. You can visit another player's profile by clicking their username anywhere on the site (leaderboards, chat, activity feed) and click the "Add Friend" button. Or, go to your own Profile, scroll to the Friends section, and search for a player by their username to send a friend request.
You earn XP (experience points) by playing games, completing daily challenges, and staying active on the site. As you accumulate XP, you level up — your current level and XP progress are displayed in the top-right corner next to your username. Higher levels show your dedication to the arcade.
Once per day, you can spin the wheel for a free coin reward. Look for the wheel icon in the top-left corner of the home page. The spin resets every 24 hours, so make sure to come back daily to collect your coins.
